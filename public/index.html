Before you draw conclusions

Test the limits.
Record what happens.

This workspace supports an experiment, not a verified anti-spoofing claim.

Manual test checklist +
  1. Enroll a consenting participant, then verify the same person under slightly different poses and lighting.
  2. Have another consenting participant claim the same test account.
  3. Try the enrolled person's phone photo held still, moved, tilted, and at different brightness levels.
  4. Try phone video from different starting points. Record every rejection and bypass.
  5. Stop the camera mid-attempt, deny or revoke permission, and test no face or multiple faces.

Record the trial ID, devices, conditions, final step, distance, and outcome. Do not share face media, descriptors, landmarks, or tokens.

What this prototype can establish +

The server checks identity distance, action order, sample tokens, and deadlines. Descriptors and landmarks still originate in a client-controlled browser. Completing the sequence does not prove a genuine camera source or resistance to photo/video attacks.

Only four action-order/direction combinations exist. Real presentation-attack trials and hardware calibration are still pending.

Data handling & resetting a session +

The camera starts only after your action and consent. No image or video is saved. Descriptors and selected landmarks are sent for matching and challenge evaluation; individual samples are not retained.

Templates cannot be freely replaced. Restart the server to clear RAM and enroll again. Hiding this page or withdrawing consent stops the camera; a request already received by the server cannot be retracted.
